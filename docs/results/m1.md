## Analyze

- **Flagger's own schema catches type mistakes, not intent.** A string port or a numeric interval
  fails the CRD schema, but a Canary pointing at a Deployment that does not exist, a port the
  container does not expose, or a Gateway that refuses the namespace is schema-valid and fails
  only during a release. The cross-reference rules exist for those.
- **The schedule is explicit.** With 15 s per step and six steps, a release reaches 80% of
  traffic after about 90 s and is promoted after about 105 s, on top of the time pods need to
  become ready. With no metrics (M1) every step passes; this is a mechanism check, not yet a
  safety check.
